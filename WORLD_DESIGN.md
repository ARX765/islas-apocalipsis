# World Design - Islas Apocalipsis

## World Overview

The world of Islas Apocalipsis is a vast collection of floating islands suspended in an endless sky. Once a unified continent, a cataclysmic event known as "The Shattering" broke the world apart, leaving fragments floating at various altitudes. These islands are held aloft by ancient magic and remnants of advanced technology.

## World Size & Structure

### Total Explorable Area
- **Size**: 5-8 km² total area
- **Altitude Range**: 0-5000m above the void
- **Major Islands**: 15-20 large landmasses
- **Minor Islands**: 40-60 smaller platforms
- **Hidden Areas**: 20-30 secret locations

### Vertical Design Philosophy
The world emphasizes vertical exploration:
- Islands at different heights
- Climbing required to access higher areas
- Aerial traversal unlocked later
- Weather varies by altitude

## World Interconnection

### Travel Methods

**Early Game:**
- Walking/Running
- Climbing
- Basic jumps between islands

**Mid Game:**
- Grappling hook unlocked
- Glide ability (from Sky Lord boss)
- Teleport crystals

**Late Game:**
- Flight ability (limited)
- Fast travel network
- Dimensional portals

### Island Connections

**Physical Bridges:**
- Ancient stone bridges
- Tech walkways
- Crystal formations

**Natural Connections:**
- Close islands for jumping
- Climbable vines between islands
- Wind currents for gliding

**Magical Connections:**
- Teleport pads
- Portal gates
- Rift passages

## Dynamic World Events

### Random Encounters
- **Merchant Balloons**: Floating shops
- **Enemy Patrols**: Roaming packs
- **Resource Nodes**: Mining/gathering
- **Mini-Challenges**: Timed events

### Time-Based Events
- **Day/Night Cycle**: 30 minutes real-time
- **Weather Patterns**: Change every 5 minutes
- **Enemy Spawns**: Different by time
- **Special Events**: Rare occurrences

## World Progression

### Gating Mechanisms
- **Required Abilities**: Climbing, gliding, magic
- **Boss Defeats**: Open new areas
- **Key Items**: Unlock doors/portals
- **Player Level**: Strong enemies guard paths

### Difficulty Zones
- **Safe Zones**: No combat (villages, checkpoints)
- **Easy Zones**: Level 1-10 enemies
- **Medium Zones**: Level 10-25 enemies
- **Hard Zones**: Level 25-35 enemies
- **Endgame Zones**: Level 35-40 enemies

## Technical Implementation

### Optimization
- **Level of Detail (LOD)**: Multiple quality levels
- **Occlusion Culling**: Hide unseen areas
- **Scene Streaming**: Load areas dynamically
- **Object Pooling**: Reuse objects

### Performance Targets
- **60 FPS**: @ 1080p medium settings
- **Draw Distance**: 500-1000m
- **Active Enemies**: 30-50 at once
- **Particle Effects**: 200-500 active

## Map System

### In-Game Map
- **Fog of War**: Reveals as explored
- **Icons**: Points of interest
- **Quest Markers**: Active objectives
- **Fast Travel**: Unlocked locations
- **Legend**: Symbol meanings

### Minimap
- **Always Visible**: Top corner
- **Rotation**: Follows player direction
- **Zoom Levels**: 3 zoom states
- **Enemy Indicators**: Red dots
- **Objective Markers**: Yellow arrows
