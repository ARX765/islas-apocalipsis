# Boss Guide - Islas Apocalipsis

## Boss Overview
All bosses have 3 phases, triggered at 70% and 40% health thresholds.

## 1. The Fallen Sky Lord
**Location**: Sky Citadel Arena  
**Level**: 15  
**Health**: 1500  
**Type**: Aerial Combat Boss

### Phase 1 (100-70% HP)
- **Attacks**: 
  - Wing Slash (melee)
  - Wind Gust (knockback)
  - Aerial Dive
- **Strategy**: Stay grounded, dodge dive attacks

### Phase 2 (70-40% HP)
- **New Attacks**:
  - Summons Sky Raiders
  - Tornado Creation
  - Lightning Strikes
- **Strategy**: Deal with adds quickly, avoid tornados

### Phase 3 (40-0% HP)
- **Enraged State**:
  - All previous attacks faster
  - Continuous aerial bombardment
  - Arena shrinks (strong winds)
- **Strategy**: Use ranged attacks, time dodges carefully

**Drops**: Sky Lord's Wings (gliding item), Wind Crystal, Rare Weapons

---

## 2. Corrupted Titan Golem
**Location**: Ancient Ruins  
**Level**: 20  
**Health**: 2000  
**Type**: Multi-part Boss

### Phase 1 (100-70% HP)
- **Weak Points**: Both Arms, Chest Core
- **Attacks**:
  - Ground Slam
  - Boulder Throw
  - Sweep Attack
- **Strategy**: Climb and attack arms, avoid ground attacks

### Phase 2 (70-40% HP)
- **Changes**: Arms destroyed, more aggressive
- **New Attacks**:
  - Charge Attack
  - Corrupted Energy Beam
  - Ground Spikes
- **Strategy**: Focus on chest core, use iframes

### Phase 3 (40-0% HP)
- **Final Form**: Core exposed, faster movement
- **New Attacks**:
  - Explosion Nova
  - Rapid Fire Energy
  - Self-Destruct Countdown
- **Strategy**: All-out damage, avoid nova

**Drops**: Titan's Core (armor upgrade), Golem Fist (weapon), Construction Plans

---

## 3. The Shadow King
**Location**: Shadow Realm  
**Level**: 25  
**Health**: 1800  
**Type**: Clone Mechanics Boss

### Phase 1 (100-70% HP)
- **Mechanics**: Creates 2 shadow clones
- **Attacks**:
  - Shadow Blade Combo
  - Teleport Strike
  - Dark Orbs
- **Strategy**: Identify real Shadow King (glowing eyes)

### Phase 2 (70-40% HP)
- **Changes**: Creates 4 clones, enters shadow realm
- **New Attacks**:
  - Realm Shift (entire arena darkens)
  - Clone Coordination Attack
  - Shadow Prison
- **Strategy**: Use light magic, break prison quickly

### Phase 3 (40-0% HP)
- **Final Phase**: All clones fuse, massive form
- **New Attacks**:
  - Reality Tear
  - Mass Teleportation
  - Ultimate Shadow Strike
- **Strategy**: Constant movement, perfect dodges

**Drops**: Shadow Crown (stealth boost), Void Blade, Shadow Essence

---

## 4. Ancient Tech Colossus
**Location**: Tech Facility Core  
**Level**: 30  
**Health**: 2200  
**Type**: Weak Point Targeting Boss

### Phase 1 (100-70% HP)
- **Weak Points**: 4 Leg Joints, Back Power Core
- **Attacks**:
  - Laser Grid
  - Missile Barrage
  - Stomp
- **Strategy**: Target legs to make it fall, attack core

### Phase 2 (70-40% HP)
- **Changes**: Hovers (legs destroyed), more aggressive
- **New Attacks**:
  - 360° Laser Sweep
  - Homing Missiles
  - EMP Pulse (disables magic)
- **Strategy**: Use cover, time attacks between sweeps

### Phase 3 (40-0% HP)
- **Overload Mode**: Core exposed, rapid firing
- **New Attacks**:
  - Overcharged Beam
  - Drone Swarm
  - Self-Repair Sequence
- **Strategy**: Interrupt repairs, focus fire on core

**Drops**: Ancient Tech Core (tech weapon upgrades), Laser Rifle, Blueprints

---

## 5. Ice Empress
**Location**: Frozen Throne  
**Level**: 28  
**Health**: 1900  
**Type**: Environmental Boss

### Phase 1 (100-70% HP)
- **Environment**: Floor freezes over time
- **Attacks**:
  - Ice Spear Throw
  - Blizzard Summon
  - Freeze Aura
- **Strategy**: Keep moving to avoid freezing, use fire magic

### Phase 2 (70-40% HP)
- **Changes**: Creates ice mirrors, reflects projectiles
- **New Attacks**:
  - Mirror Clone Attacks
  - Ice Prison
  - Hailstorm
- **Strategy**: Break mirrors, close combat preferred

### Phase 3 (40-0% HP)
- **Enraged**: Entire arena becomes frozen
- **New Attacks**:
  - Absolute Zero (arena-wide freeze)
  - Ice Meteor Rain
  - Frozen Time (slow effect)
- **Strategy**: Use stamina wisely, resist ice with fire buffs

**Drops**: Crown of Winter (ice magic boost), Frozen Heart, Ice Scepter

---

## 6. Fire Drake
**Location**: Volcanic Peak  
**Level**: 32  
**Health**: 2100  
**Type**: Flying Dragon Boss

### Phase 1 (100-70% HP)
- **Pattern**: Flies and lands periodically
- **Attacks**:
  - Fire Breath
  - Tail Swipe
  - Claw Slash
- **Strategy**: Attack when grounded, dodge breath

### Phase 2 (70-40% HP)
- **Changes**: Stays airborne longer, arena catches fire
- **New Attacks**:
  - Dive Bomb
  - Fire Ball Barrage
  - Flame Tornado
- **Strategy**: Use ranged attacks, manage stamina for dodges

### Phase 3 (40-0% HP)
- **Inferno Mode**: Entire body on fire, arena is lava
- **New Attacks**:
  - Meteor Strike
  - Continuous Fire Breath
  - Ring of Fire
- **Strategy**: Use ice magic, stay on safe platforms

**Drops**: Drake's Heart (fire resistance), Dragon Scale Armor, Fire Wings

---

## 7. Storm Sovereign
**Location**: Lightning Spire  
**Level**: 35  
**Health**: 2000  
**Type**: Weather Control Boss

### Phase 1 (100-70% HP)
- **Weather**: Light rain, occasional lightning
- **Attacks**:
  - Lightning Bolt
  - Thunder Clap
  - Electric Field
- **Strategy**: Avoid puddles (conduct electricity), stay mobile

### Phase 2 (70-40% HP)
- **Weather**: Heavy storm, reduced visibility
- **New Attacks**:
  - Chain Lightning
  - Storm Summon (enemies)
  - Tornado
- **Strategy**: Deal with summons, use earth magic for grounding

### Phase 3 (40-0% HP)
- **Enraged**: Constant lightning strikes, hurricane winds
- **New Attacks**:
  - Zeus's Wrath (targeted lightning)
  - Thunder Dome
  - Lightning Form (becomes pure energy)
- **Strategy**: Time attacks between lightning strikes, dodge everything

**Drops**: Storm Crown (lightning magic), Thunder Hammer, Weather Rune

---

## 8. The Void Entity
**Location**: Void Rift  
**Level**: 38  
**Health**: 2300  
**Type**: Reality Warping Boss

### Phase 1 (100-70% HP)
- **Mechanics**: Reality distortions, gravity shifts
- **Attacks**:
  - Void Tentacles
  - Reality Slash
  - Gravity Well
- **Strategy**: Adapt to gravity changes, cut tentacles

### Phase 2 (70-40% HP)
- **Changes**: Arena splits into platforms, void between
- **New Attacks**:
  - Dimensional Rift (summons void creatures)
  - Existence Erasure
  - Time Distortion
- **Strategy**: Platform hop, prioritize void creatures

### Phase 3 (40-0% HP)
- **Final Form**: Reveals true form, massive
- **New Attacks**:
  - Void Storm
  - Reality Collapse
  - End of All Things (wipe mechanic)
- **Strategy**: Maximum DPS, interrupt wipe mechanic

**Drops**: Void Heart (unique armor), Reality Blade, Dimensional Key

---

## 9. Crystal Hivemind
**Location**: Crystal Caverns  
**Level**: 36  
**Health**: Multiple forms - 500 HP each  
**Type**: Multi-form Boss

### Phase 1 (Form 1)
- **Mechanic**: Main crystal + 4 smaller crystals
- **Attacks**:
  - Crystal Shard Projectiles
  - Laser Beam
  - Crystal Growth (terrain hazard)
- **Strategy**: Destroy smaller crystals first

### Phase 2 (Form 2)
- **Changes**: Forms into humanoid shape
- **New Attacks**:
  - Crystal Sword Combos
  - Reflective Shield
  - Crystal Summons
- **Strategy**: Break shield, interrupt summons

### Phase 3 (Form 3)
- **Final Form**: Swarm of crystals
- **New Attacks**:
  - Crystal Storm
  - Hivemind Coordination
  - Crystal Prison
- **Strategy**: AOE attacks, constant movement

**Drops**: Hivemind Core (magic boost), Crystal Weapons, Pure Crystal

---

## 10. The Last Guardian - Final Boss
**Location**: The Core  
**Level**: 40  
**Health**: 3000  
**Type**: Ultimate Multi-phase Boss

### Phase 1 (100-75% HP)
- **Uses**: Combination of all previous boss mechanics
- **Attacks**:
  - Weapon Mastery (all weapon types)
  - Basic Magic (all elements)
  - Guardian Shield
- **Strategy**: Use all learned skills, perfect timing

### Phase 2 (75-50% HP)
- **Transformation**: Absorbs power, gains wings
- **New Attacks**:
  - Advanced Magic Combinations
  - Time Manipulation
  - Reality Bending
- **Strategy**: Adapt quickly, use best gear

### Phase 3 (50-25% HP)
- **Ascension**: Becomes pure energy
- **New Attacks**:
  - Apocalypse Beam
  - Summons All Boss Types (mini versions)
  - Arena Transformation
- **Strategy**: Manage adds, save resources

### Phase 4 (25-0% HP)
- **Final Stand**: All mechanics active
- **New Attacks**:
  - True Guardian Form
  - End Cycle (wipe after timer)
  - Ultimate Combination Attacks
- **Strategy**: Perfect execution, maximum DPS

**Drops**: Guardian's Legacy (best armor), Truth Seeker (best weapon), Ending Unlocks

---

## General Boss Tips
1. Learn attack patterns in Phase 1
2. Save healing items for Phase 3
3. Use appropriate elemental resistances
4. Lock-on for better tracking
5. Manage stamina carefully
6. Watch for phase transition tells
7. Break boss armor/weak points when possible
8. Use environment to your advantage
9. Perfect dodge for slow-mo opportunities
10. Don't get greedy with attacks
