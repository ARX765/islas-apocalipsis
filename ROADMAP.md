## Risk Management

### High Risk Items
1. **Scope creep**: Stick to documented design
2. **Performance**: Regular profiling and optimization
3. **Content volume**: Prioritize core content first
4. **Team size**: May need to adjust timeline or scope

### Mitigation Strategies
- Regular milestone reviews
- Flexible timeline with buffer
- Core gameplay first, polish later
- Community feedback integration
- Iterative development approach
