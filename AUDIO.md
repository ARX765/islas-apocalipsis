# Audio Design - Islas Apocalipsis

## Music Tracks

### Main Menu
- **Main Theme** (3:30)
  - Epic orchestral with medieval instruments
  - Post-apocalyptic atmosphere
  - Looping

### Exploration Music

#### Region Themes (each 4-6 minutes, looping)
1. **Starting Highlands** - Hopeful, adventurous
2. **Tech Ruins** - Mysterious, electronic undertones
3. **Crystal Caverns** - Ethereal, echoing
4. **Scorched Wastes** - Intense, drums heavy
5. **Frozen Peaks** - Quiet, wind instruments
6. **Storm Islands** - Thunder ambience, tense
7. **Shadow Realm** - Dark, ominous choir
8. **Sky Gardens** - Peaceful, nature sounds
9. **Void Rifts** - Distorted, reality-bending
10. **The Core** - All themes combined

### Combat Music

#### Dynamic Combat System
- **Low Intensity** (1-2 enemies)
  - Light percussion
  - Gradually builds
  
- **Medium Intensity** (3-5 enemies)
  - Full drums
  - Strings intensify
  
- **High Intensity** (6+ enemies or elite)
  - Full orchestra
  - Epic chorus

### Boss Battle Themes (each 5-7 minutes)

1. **The Fallen Sky Lord** - Aerial, wind instruments dominant
2. **Corrupted Titan Golem** - Heavy percussion, mechanical sounds
3. **The Shadow King** - Dark choir, haunting melody
4. **Ancient Tech Colossus** - Electronic/orchestral fusion
5. **Ice Empress** - Cold, crystalline sounds
6. **Fire Drake** - Intense drums, roaring brass
7. **Storm Sovereign** - Thunder, electric guitar elements
8. **The Void Entity** - Reality-warping, dissonant
9. **Crystal Hivemind** - Crystalline resonance, eerie

- Phase 1: Introduction (0-70% HP)

## Audio Implementation

### Technical Specs
- **Format**: WAV (uncompressed), OGG (compressed)
- **Sample Rate**: 44.1kHz
- **Bit Depth**: 16-bit
- **Channels**: Stereo (music), Mono (SFX)

### Audio Categories
1. **Music**: 
   - Volume control
   - Crossfade between tracks
   - Combat dynamic layering

2. **SFX**:
   - 3D spatial audio
   - Distance attenuation
   - Occlusion

3. **Ambience**:
   - Looping layers
   - Weather integration
   - Location blending

4. **Voice** (if applicable):
   - Character grunts
   - Boss taunts
   - NPC dialogue

### Audio Mixing
- **Music**: -12dB to -6dB
- **SFX**: -6dB to 0dB (peaks)
- **Ambience**: -18dB to -12dB
- **UI**: -9dB to -6dB

### Priority System
1. Critical UI sounds
2. Player combat sounds
3. Enemy sounds (nearby)
4. Magic effects
5. Environmental sounds
6. Distant sounds
7. Ambient layers

## Audio Budget
- **Total SFX**: ~500-800 unique sounds
- **Music Tracks**: 30-40 tracks
- **Total Audio Size**: ~2-3 GB

## Accessibility Options
- Volume sliders (Music, SFX, Ambience, UI)
- Subtitles for important audio cues
- Visual indicators for directional sounds
- Screen reader support for UI
