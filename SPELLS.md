## Advanced Combos

### Fire + Ice = Steam Explosion
- **Damage**: 100 AOE
- **Combo**: Cast Fireball then Ice Bolt at same location
- **Effect**: Massive AOE damage

### Lightning + Water = Conductivity
- **Damage**: Chain damage x2
- **Combo**: Lightning spells on wet enemies (rain/water)
- **Effect**: Double chain range

### Wind + Fire = Firestorm
- **Damage**: 60/sec
- **Combo**: Tornado + Fire Wall
- **Effect**: Moving fire tornado

### Earth + Shadow = Corrupted Ground
- **Damage**: 35/sec + DOT
- **Combo**: Earthquake + Void Zone
- **Effect**: Persistent damage field

### Light + Time = Eternal Moment
- **Effect**: Buff duration x2
- **Combo**: Any buff + Haste
- **Effect**: Extended buff time

## Spell Upgrade System

### Spell Levels (1-5)
- **Level 1**: Base spell
- **Level 2**: +20% damage/effect
- **Level 3**: +40% damage/effect, -10% mana cost
- **Level 4**: +60% damage/effect, -20% mana cost
- **Level 5**: +100% damage/effect, -30% mana cost, special bonus

### Upgrade Materials
- **Tier 1**: Magic Crystals
- **Tier 2**: Elemental Essences
- **Tier 3**: Rare Spell Scrolls
- **Tier 4**: Boss Magic Cores
- **Tier 5**: Legendary Artifacts
