## Enemy Spawning Guidelines

### Difficulty Scaling
- **Easy Areas**: Common enemies (1-20)
- **Medium Areas**: Mix of common and elite (1-30)
- **Hard Areas**: Elite and special enemies (21-40)
- **Boss Areas**: All types possible

### Pack Composition
- **Standard Pack**: 3-5 common enemies
- **Elite Pack**: 1-2 elite + 2-4 common
- **Special Encounter**: 1 special + support enemies
- **Challenge Pack**: Mix of all types
