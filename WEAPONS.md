## Weapon Upgrade System

All weapons can be upgraded at blacksmiths:

### Upgrade Tiers
1. **+1 to +3**: +10% damage per level
2. **+4 to +6**: +15% damage per level
3. **+7 to +9**: +20% damage per level
4. **+10 (Max)**: +30% damage, special effect unlock

### Upgrade Materials
- **Common**: Metal Scraps, Wood
- **Uncommon**: Ores, Crystals
- **Rare**: Boss Materials, Essences
- **Legendary**: Unique Boss Drops

## Weapon Combos

### Light Attack Chains
- 1-Hit: Basic damage
- 2-Hit: +20% damage
- 3-Hit: +40% damage + knockback

### Heavy Attack
- Charged: +150% damage
- Breaks enemy guard
- Higher stamina cost

### Special Combos
- **Light x2 + Heavy**: Launcher combo
- **Heavy + Light x3**: Destruction combo
- **Jump + Heavy**: Plunge attack
- **Dodge + Light**: Quick strike
- **Parry + Heavy**: Critical counter
